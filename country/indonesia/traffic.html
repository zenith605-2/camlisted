About traffic cams in Indonesia

These 73 feeds come from 15 channels, led by Kominfo Gunungkidul (18), CCTV Pemerintah Kabupaten Bantul (10), and Dinas Perhubungan Kabupaten Magelang (8) — a mix of operators running multiple cameras and one-off contributors, which is why framing varies across the page.

Traffic makes up 74% of the 98 cameras Camlisted currently tracks in Indonesia, so this page is a slice of that catalogue rather than the whole of it.

Indonesia runs on UTC+07:00, so the overnight stretch that yields low-light footage falls roughly between 15:00 and 22:00 UTC — worth knowing if you are after night scenes rather than daylight.

Resolution figures here are measured, not scraped from titles: Camlisted records the real playback quality when a feed is opened. 56 of these feeds have been measured so far — 3 in 4K, 6 in QHD, 24 in full HD, and 13 in HD.

The longest-running live camera in this set has been broadcasting continuously since 2025 — “LIVE PEMANTAUAN CCTV S3 PETOYAN 2”.
