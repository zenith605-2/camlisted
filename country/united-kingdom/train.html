About train / railway cams in United Kingdom

These 35 feeds come from 12 channels, led by RailcamLive - WSR (8), Severn Valley Railway (6), and Railcam Live-NG-Railways (6) — a mix of operators running multiple cameras and one-off contributors, which is why framing varies across the page.

Train / Railway makes up 24% of the 146 cameras Camlisted currently tracks in United Kingdom, so this page is a slice of that catalogue rather than the whole of it.

United Kingdom runs on UTC+01:00, so the overnight stretch that yields low-light footage falls roughly between 21:00 and 04:00 UTC — worth knowing if you are after night scenes rather than daylight.

Resolution figures here are measured, not scraped from titles: Camlisted records the real playback quality when a feed is opened. 30 of these feeds have been measured so far — 3 in QHD, 18 in full HD, and 9 in HD.

The longest-running live camera in this set has been broadcasting continuously since 2024 — “Teign Estuary Cam @ Teignmouth - Railcam UK”.
