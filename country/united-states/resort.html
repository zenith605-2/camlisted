United States runs on UTC−04:00, so the overnight stretch that yields low-light footage falls roughly between 02:00 and 09:00 UTC — worth knowing if you are after night scenes rather than daylight.

Resolution figures here are measured, not scraped from titles: Camlisted records the real playback quality when a feed is opened. 32 of these feeds have been measured so far — 4 in 4K, 19 in full HD, and 8 in HD.
