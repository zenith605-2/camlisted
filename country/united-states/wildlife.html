About wildlife cams in United States

These 95 feeds come from 46 channels, led by PixCams (21), Explore Eagle Nest Cams (7), and Explore Oceans (4) — a mix of operators running multiple cameras and one-off contributors, which is why framing varies across the page.

Wildlife makes up 9% of the 1043 cameras Camlisted currently tracks in United States, so this page is a slice of that catalogue rather than the whole of it.

United States runs on UTC−04:00, so the overnight stretch that yields low-light footage falls roughly between 02:00 and 09:00 UTC — worth knowing if you are after night scenes rather than daylight.

Resolution figures here are measured, not scraped from titles: Camlisted records the real playback quality when a feed is opened. 80 of these feeds have been measured so far — 5 in 4K, 10 in QHD, 58 in full HD, and 7 in HD.

Condition tags mark what a clip actually shows, so you can jump straight to a scenario: currently 2 night scenes and 1 fire scene tagged in United States.

The longest-running live camera in this set has been broadcasting continuously since 2018 — “Otter Outdoor Cam”.
