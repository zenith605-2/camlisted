About harbor cams in United States

These 66 feeds come from 39 channels, led by Duluth Harbor Cam (7), StreamTime Live (4), and San Diego Web Cam (4) — a mix of operators running multiple cameras and one-off contributors, which is why framing varies across the page.

Harbor makes up 6% of the 1020 cameras Camlisted currently tracks in United States, so this page is a slice of that catalogue rather than the whole of it.

United States runs on UTC−04:00, so the overnight stretch that yields low-light footage falls roughly between 02:00 and 09:00 UTC — worth knowing if you are after night scenes rather than daylight.

Resolution figures here are measured, not scraped from titles: Camlisted records the real playback quality when a feed is opened. 53 of these feeds have been measured so far — 3 in 4K, 4 in QHD, 35 in full HD, and 11 in HD.

The longest-running live camera in this set has been broadcasting continuously since 2019 — “Morro Bay Yacht Club - See.Cam”.
