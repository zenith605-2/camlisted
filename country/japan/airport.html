About airport cams in Japan

These 32 feeds come from 26 channels, led by 朝日新聞LIVE (5), Japan 8K12K TV (2), and 福岡TNC ニュース (2) — a mix of operators running multiple cameras and one-off contributors, which is why framing varies across the page.

Airport makes up 4% of the 821 cameras Camlisted currently tracks in Japan, so this page is a slice of that catalogue rather than the whole of it.

Japan runs on UTC+09:00, so the overnight stretch that yields low-light footage falls roughly between 13:00 and 20:00 UTC — worth knowing if you are after night scenes rather than daylight.

Resolution figures here are measured, not scraped from titles: Camlisted records the real playback quality when a feed is opened. 23 of these feeds have been measured so far — 19 in full HD and 4 in HD.

The longest-running live camera in this set has been broadcasting continuously since 2020 — “いわて花巻空港ライブ映像”.
