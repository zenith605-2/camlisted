Japan runs on UTC+09:00, so the overnight stretch that yields low-light footage falls roughly between 13:00 and 20:00 UTC — worth knowing if you are after night scenes rather than daylight.

Resolution figures here are measured, not scraped from titles: Camlisted records the real playback quality when a feed is opened. 36 of these feeds have been measured so far — 1 in 4K, 21 in full HD, and 10 in HD.
