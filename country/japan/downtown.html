About street cams in Japan

Street makes up 6% of the 832 cameras Camlisted currently tracks in Japan, so this page is a slice of that catalogue rather than the whole of it.

Japan runs on UTC+09:00, so the overnight stretch that yields low-light footage falls roughly between 13:00 and 20:00 UTC — worth knowing if you are after night scenes rather than daylight.

Resolution figures here are measured, not scraped from titles: Camlisted records the real playback quality when a feed is opened. 30 of these feeds have been measured so far — 1 in 4K, 5 in QHD, 11 in full HD, and 2 in HD.

Condition tags mark what a clip actually shows, so you can jump straight to a scenario: currently 6 night scenes and 1 snow scene tagged in Japan.
