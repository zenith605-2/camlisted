About avenue cams

Avenue cams frame the long straight view — boulevards, main streets, tree-lined approaches where traffic and pedestrians share the frame. They sit between a traffic cam and a downtown cam: enough vehicles to be useful, enough sidewalk life to be watchable.

Much of this collection comes from dedicated operators — JazBaz Philippines (15 feeds), Randomly Entertained (10 feeds), and The Real Samui Webcam (6 feeds) — channels that keep cameras running year-round rather than posting one-off clips.

Resolution figures here are measured, not scraped from titles: Camlisted records the real playback quality when a feed is opened. 111 of these feeds have been measured so far — 8 in 4K, 14 in QHD, 71 in full HD, and 13 in HD.

Condition tags mark what a clip actually shows, so you can jump straight to a scenario: currently 7 night scenes and 1 rain scene tagged here.

The longest-running live camera in this set has been broadcasting continuously since 2018 — “Renesse aan Zee”.

Frequently asked questions

How many avenue live cams are there?

124 live avenue cameras are streaming right now, plus 24 recorded clips, with the deepest coverage in United States, Japan, Philippines, and Thailand. The list is re-verified every night.

What are avenue cams useful for?

Beyond watching for its own sake, fixed public cameras of this kind are widely used as reference and test footage — checking how detection or tracking models behave on real scenes instead of curated datasets.

Is it free to watch? Do I need an account?

Yes, and no account is needed. Every feed is a public YouTube stream embedded from its original channel — Camlisted links to sources and never hosts or downloads video.
