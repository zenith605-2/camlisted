About beach cams

Beach cams are the comfort food of the live-cam world: waves on a loop, weather you can read at a glance, and a horizon that resets your attention span. Surfers use them to check swell before driving out, expats use them to look in on a hometown shore, and plenty of people just leave one open as a window they don't have. They are also the easiest way to compare seasons — the same strip of sand in July and January is two different worlds.

Much of this collection comes from dedicated operators — Ozolio Plus (10 feeds), Mallorca Webcam (9 feeds), and Elbo Room (Home of ElboTV) (8 feeds) — channels that keep cameras running year-round rather than posting one-off clips.

Resolution figures here are measured, not scraped from titles: Camlisted records the real playback quality when a feed is opened. 211 of these feeds have been measured so far — 40 in 4K, 14 in QHD, 117 in full HD, and 31 in HD.

Condition tags mark what a clip actually shows, so you can jump straight to a scenario: currently 7 night scenes and 1 rain scene tagged here.

Frequently asked questions

How many beach live cams are there?

244 live beach cameras are streaming right now, plus 30 recorded clips, with the deepest coverage in United States, Spain, Japan, and Brazil. The list is re-verified every night.

What are beach cams useful for?

Beyond watching for its own sake, fixed public cameras of this kind are widely used as reference and test footage — checking how detection or tracking models behave on real scenes instead of curated datasets.

Is it free to watch? Do I need an account?

Yes, and no account is needed. Every feed is a public YouTube stream embedded from its original channel — Camlisted links to sources and never hosts or downloads video.
