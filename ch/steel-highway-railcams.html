About Steel Highway Railcams

Steel Highway Railcams runs all 26 of its cameras in United States, so this page doubles as a local view of that one country rather than a world tour.

By subject the set leans towards train / railway (26) — a single-theme operator. Camlisted files each camera by what it actually shows, and signed-in visitors can correct that classification when it looks wrong.

Every one of these 26 entries is a continuously running live stream rather than a recorded upload, which is the pattern of an operator maintaining fixed cameras rather than posting clips.

Resolution figures here are measured, not scraped from titles: Camlisted records the real playback quality when a feed is opened. 23 of these feeds have been measured so far — 1 in QHD, 17 in full HD, and 5 in HD.
