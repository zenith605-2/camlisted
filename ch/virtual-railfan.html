About Virtual Railfan

Virtual Railfan spreads its cameras across 2 countries — mostly United States (57) and Canada (1). That puts it in the minority here: 80% of the operators Camlisted indexes keep every camera inside a single country.

By subject the set leans towards train / railway (57) and parking (1). Camlisted files each camera by what it actually shows, and signed-in visitors can correct that classification when it looks wrong.

Every one of these 58 entries is a continuously running live stream rather than a recorded upload, which is the pattern of an operator maintaining fixed cameras rather than posting clips.

Resolution figures here are measured, not scraped from titles: Camlisted records the real playback quality when a feed is opened. 51 of these feeds have been measured so far — 1 in 4K and 50 in full HD.

The longest-running live camera in this set has been broadcasting continuously since 2024 — “Fort Madison, Iowa, USA | LIVE Train Camera (PTZ)”.
